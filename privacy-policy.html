Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

At AR Tours, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website and book our luxury chauffeur and private tour services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when booking or inquiring about our services. This includes:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Booking Details: Pick-up locations, destination details, passenger count, child safety seat requirements, flight details, and tour selections.
  • Payment Details: We process payments securely and do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, manage, and deliver our premium services, including:

  • Processing and confirming your tour and transfer reservations.
  • Communicating with you regarding booking confirmations, pick-up times, and service updates.
  • Customizing your private tour itineraries.
  • Complying with local transport safety and licensing regulations.

3. Protecting Your Information

We implement a variety of security measures, including SSL encryption, access control, and secure firewall structures to maintain the safety of your personal information. We will never sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to outside parties, except trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or serving you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

4. Direct Communications

By submitting your phone number via our booking form, you consent to receive direct contact (calls, SMS, or WhatsApp) from our concierge team solely to coordinate your itinerary, vehicle matching, or reservation adjustments.
