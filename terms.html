All bookings submitted through our website are **requests for reservation** and do not represent real-time availability. Your booking is only confirmed once our concierge team has contacted you (via email, phone, or WhatsApp) and sent a formal confirmation notice alongside a payment invoice.

2. Pricing & Payments

  • All prices quoted on the website are in Australian Dollars (AUD) and are inclusive of GST.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that travel plans can change. Our refund rules are as follows:

  • Cancellations made 48 hours or more prior to the scheduled departure time are eligible for a full (100%) refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours prior to the departure time are non-refundable.
  • No-shows on the day of travel will be charged in full (100%).

We maintain the highest standards of cleanliness and safety. Smoking, vaping, and the consumption of food are strictly prohibited inside all fleet vehicles. Any damage caused to the vehicle interior or exterior by passengers will incur a full repair and cleaning fee starting at $350 AUD.

While we do everything possible to maintain scheduled timings, AR Tours is not liable for service delays caused by heavy traffic congestion, road closures, adverse weather conditions, or flight delays beyond our control. In such cases, your guide will alter the itinerary route to ensure passenger safety and convenience.
