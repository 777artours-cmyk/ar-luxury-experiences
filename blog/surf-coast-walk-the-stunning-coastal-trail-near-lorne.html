What is the Surf Coast Walk?

The Surf Coast Walk is a celebrated 44-kilometre trail that meanders from Point Addis, just south of Torquay, all the way to Fairhaven, near Aireys Inlet. While its full length offers an epic multi-day challenge, it’s designed to be enjoyed in smaller, accessible sections, making it perfect for day-trippers and casual strollers alike. Each segment of the walk unveils a new facet of the region's diverse landscape – from rugged clifftop paths boasting panoramic ocean vistas to secluded beaches, lush coastal forests, and vibrant estuary systems. It’s a mosaic of natural wonders, offering something for every pace and preference.

Highlights of Your Coastal Journey

  • Aireys Inlet & Split Point Lighthouse: Begin your adventure or explore this iconic section, dominated by the striking red and white striped Split Point Lighthouse. Ascend for unparalleled panoramic views of the coastline, stretching from Lorne to Bells Beach. The surrounding cliffs and hidden coves are a photographer's dream.
  • Anglesea's Natural Beauty: Discover the tranquil beauty of the Anglesea River estuary, a haven for birdlife. Keep an eye out for kangaroos often seen grazing nonchalantly on the Anglesea Golf Course – a truly Australian sight! The paths here offer gentle riverside strolls and stunning ocean outlooks.
  • World-Famous Bells Beach: Even if you're not a surfer, a visit to Bells Beach is a must. Stand atop the cliffs and feel the raw power of the Southern Ocean as waves crash against the shore. This legendary surfing Mecca hosts the Rip Curl Pro and offers an exhilarating spectacle of nature's force.
  • Lorne's Charm: As a gateway or endpoint to many sections of the walk, Lorne itself is a vibrant coastal town brimming with cafes, galleries, and a relaxed atmosphere. It's the perfect spot to unwind, enjoy a gourmet meal, or simply soak in the seaside ambiance after your walk.

An Experience for Every Explorer

Whether you're a seasoned hiker eager to conquer challenging terrain or simply seeking a leisurely stroll with magnificent views, the Surf Coast Walk caters to all. The air is crisp and salty, invigorating your senses with every step. Opportunities for photography abound, capturing dramatic sunsets, crashing waves, unique flora, and perhaps even glimpses of marine life like dolphins or whales during migration season. Birdwatchers will delight in the diverse avian species calling this coast home, while those seeking serenity will find it in abundance amidst the natural grandeur.

Planning Your Surf Coast Walk Experience

The Surf Coast Walk is beautiful year-round, but it truly shines during the milder months of spring and autumn, offering pleasant temperatures for walking. Always come prepared with layers of clothing, sturdy walking shoes, sun protection, and plenty of water. While the full 44km requires dedicated planning, many shorter, well-maintained sections are perfect for a half-day or full-day excursion. Your AR Tours chauffeur can advise on the best sections to suit your fitness level and interests, ensuring a perfect day out.
