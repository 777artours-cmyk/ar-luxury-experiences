The Iconic Great Ocean Road: Beyond the Twelve Apostles

No discussion of Victorian coastal beauty is complete without mentioning the legendary Great Ocean Road. While famous for its dramatic Twelve Apostles, the region boasts numerous walks that offer intimate encounters with its majestic landscapes. AR Tours provides the perfect vantage point, allowing you to savour every panoramic vista without the distraction of driving.

  • Great Ocean Walk (Sections): For the avid walker, select sections of the 104km Great Ocean Walk offer unparalleled immersion. Consider the stretch from Apollo Bay to Shelly Beach, a moderate 8.5km walk through eucalyptus forests opening onto stunning ocean views. Alternatively, the path leading to the Twelve Apostles, while popular, offers unique perspectives away from the main viewing platforms. Your AR Tours chauffeur will drop you at the trailhead and be ready to whisk you away to a gourmet lunch or a comfortable return journey afterwards.
  • Loch Ard Gorge to The Arch: A shorter, yet incredibly dramatic walk, this path takes you through a series of stunning rock formations, blowholes, and serene coves. The history of the Loch Ard shipwreck adds a poignant layer to the natural beauty. With AR Tours, you can explore these wonders at your own pace, knowing every detail of your transport is meticulously managed. To explore more of this iconic region, consider our tailored Great Ocean Road tours.

Mornington Peninsula: Coastal Charm and Culinary Delights

Just an hour's drive from Melbourne, the Mornington Peninsula is a haven of rolling vineyards, charming seaside towns, and stunning coastal trails. It's the perfect destination for a day trip combining invigorating walks with indulgent experiences, all made effortless with AR Tours.

  • Millionaire's Walk, Sorrento: A relatively easy 1.5km clifftop path offering spectacular views of Port Phillip Bay and the impressive mansions below. This walk provides a glimpse into the Peninsula's exclusive side, blending natural beauty with sophisticated charm. It's an ideal pre-lunch stroll before heading to one of the region's acclaimed wineries or restaurants.
  • Cape Schanck Lighthouse Walk: Discover rugged beauty at the southernmost tip of the Mornington Peninsula. The walk to the historic lighthouse offers breathtaking views of Bass Strait and the dramatic volcanic rock formations. The boardwalks and stairs are well-maintained, making it accessible for most fitness levels. AR Tours ensures your journey to and from this remote yet rewarding location is smooth and stress-free.

Phillip Island: Wildlife, Waves, and Wild Coastlines

Renowned for its Penguin Parade, Phillip Island also boasts incredible coastal walks that showcase its diverse natural beauty, from windswept beaches to ancient basalt columns. AR Tours can seamlessly integrate a coastal walk into your wildlife viewing itinerary, offering a truly comprehensive experience.

  • Cape Woolamai Circuit: This 8km circuit is considered one of Phillip Island's best walks, featuring stunning clifftop views, an ancient granite quarry, and the iconic Pinnacles rock formation. It offers panoramic vistas of the rugged coastline and Bass Strait, making it a photographer's dream. The varying terrain keeps the walk engaging, and your private chauffeur will be ready to greet you with refreshments upon your return.
  • Nobbies Centre to Penguin Parade Coastal Walk: While shorter, this walk offers spectacular views of the Nobbies rock formations and Seal Rocks, home to Australia's largest fur seal colony. It's a fantastic prelude to the Penguin Parade, allowing you to soak in the coastal atmosphere before the evening spectacle. AR Tours ensures you arrive at the perfect time, relaxed and ready for the wonders ahead.
