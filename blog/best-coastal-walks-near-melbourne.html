Introduction to Melbourne's Coastal Walks

Melbourne, the cultural and sporting capital of Australia, is surrounded by a stunning coastline that offers some of the most breathtaking and diverse landscapes in the country. From rugged cliffs to secluded beaches, the coastal walks near Melbourne are a must-do for any nature lover or outdoor enthusiast.

Top Coastal Walks Near Melbourne

Some of the top coastal walks near Melbourne include:

  • St Kilda Pier to Brighton Beach Walk: A scenic 8km walk along the beachfront, offering stunning views of Port Phillip Bay and the city skyline.
  • Sorrento to Portsea Walk: A 4km walk along the clifftops, offering breathtaking views of the Bass Strait and the surrounding coastline.
  • Great Ocean Road Walk: A 100km walk that stretches from Torquay to Apollo Bay, offering some of the most spectacular coastal scenery in Australia.
