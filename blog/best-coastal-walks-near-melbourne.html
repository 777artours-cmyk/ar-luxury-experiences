The Iconic Great Ocean Road: A World-Class Walking Experience

No discussion of coastal walks near Melbourne is complete without mentioning the legendary Great Ocean Road. This engineering marvel is not just a scenic drive; it's a gateway to incredible walking trails that unveil the raw beauty of Victoria's coastline. Imagine standing atop a cliff, the salty breeze invigorating your senses, as you gaze upon the magnificent Twelve Apostles. While the full Great Ocean Walk spans over 100km, many shorter, accessible sections offer equally breathtaking views.

  • Port Campbell National Park: Explore the paths around the Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge, and Gibson Steps. These walks are relatively short but incredibly impactful, offering close-up encounters with towering limestone stacks and dramatic erosion formations.
  • Kennett River to Apollo Bay: For a more immersive experience, sections of the Great Ocean Walk like this offer stunning rainforest backdrops meeting ocean views, perfect for spotting koalas in the wild.

Mornington Peninsula: Refined Coastal Charm & Bay Views

Just an hour south-east of Melbourne, the Mornington Peninsula offers a different, yet equally captivating, coastal walking experience. Here, rolling hills meet pristine beaches, and charming seaside towns are nestled alongside rugged clifftops. It's a region where natural beauty blends seamlessly with gourmet delights and sophisticated relaxation.

  • Cape Schanck Lighthouse Walk: A truly dramatic walk at the southern tip of the peninsula. The boardwalk trails lead to the historic lighthouse, offering panoramic views of Bass Strait and the rugged coastline. The unique rock formations and tidal pools are a photographer's dream.
  • Millionaire's Walk (Sorrento): A relatively easy, yet incredibly scenic clifftop path offering breathtaking views across Port Phillip Bay, showcasing some of Victoria's most exclusive properties.
  • Point Nepean National Park: Explore historical military fortifications and enjoy stunning bay and ocean views from the very tip of the peninsula. The paved roads and walking tracks make it accessible for all fitness levels.

AR Tours provides the ultimate convenience for exploring the Mornington Peninsula. Combine a refreshing coastal walk with a visit to a world-class winery, a dip in the Peninsula Hot Springs, or a gourmet lunch at a celebrated restaurant. Our private chauffeurs ensure your day is perfectly paced and utterly indulgent.

Phillip Island: Wildlife Wonders & Dramatic Coastlines

An hour and a half drive from Melbourne, Phillip Island is renowned for its incredible wildlife, particularly the nightly Penguin Parade. However, it also boasts spectacular coastal walks that offer dramatic scenery and opportunities to spot local fauna in their natural habitat.

  • Cape Woolamai Circuit: This impressive walk offers some of the most spectacular coastal scenery on the island, featuring towering granite cliffs, sandy beaches, and vibrant pink granite formations. Choose from shorter loops or the full 7km circuit.
  • The Nobbies Centre & Boardwalk: An easy, accessible boardwalk offering stunning views of the rugged coastline, home to a large colony of fur seals (visible from a distance) and a blowhole that puts on a show in rough seas.

Experience Phillip Island's natural beauty with AR Tours. Our private chauffeurs will ensure you arrive in comfort, allowing you to enjoy the walks and then be perfectly positioned for the evening Penguin Parade, free from the stresses of parking and navigating.

Wilson's Promontory National Park: A Wilderness Escape

While a bit further afield (approximately 3 hours south-east of Melbourne), Wilson's Promontory, affectionately known as "The Prom," is an absolute must for serious walkers and nature enthusiasts. This pristine national park offers an unparalleled wilderness experience with granite mountains, untouched beaches, and diverse wildlife.

  • Squeaky Beach: Famous for its pure white quartz sand that "squeaks" underfoot, this iconic beach is accessible via a short walk and offers stunning photographic opportunities.
  • Mount Oberon Summit Walk: A challenging but incredibly rewarding climb offering panoramic views of the entire Promontory, including Tidal River and the surrounding coastline.

For those seeking a truly immersive multi-day coastal walking adventure, AR Tours can tailor a luxury itinerary to Wilson's Promontory. Enjoy the rugged beauty during the day, then retreat to premium accommodation, knowing every detail of your transport has been meticulously handled.
