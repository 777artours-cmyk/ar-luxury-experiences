By AR Tours·October 13, 2026·8 min read
Back to Blog

Welcome to Melbourne in Style

Melbourne, Australia’s cultural capital, blends world‑class dining, avant‑garde art, and pristine natural beauty. For discerning travelers, the city offers a curated palette of experiences that go beyond the typical tourist trail.

Hidden Gems & Fascinating Facts

While the bustling laneways and the iconic Flinders Street Station are must‑sees, the true luxury lies in discovering what most visitors miss.

Fact #1

Melbourne’s tram network is the largest in the world outside of Europe, boasting 24‑hour service on weekends—a perfect way to glide through the city in comfort.

Fact #2

The underground laneway art scene features over 200 ever‑changing murals, many hidden behind unmarked doors that only locals know.

A Brief History

Founded in 1835 and catapulted to prominence during the 1850s gold rush, Melbourne quickly became a hub of wealth and culture. Its Victorian architecture and grand boulevards still echo that era of opulence.

Luxury Travel Tips

Tip #1: Private Yarra River Yacht

Book a vintage‑styled charter for a sunset cruise, complete with a sommelier‑curated tasting menu and panoramic city views.

Tip #2: Stay at The Langham

Reserve the Executive Suite with a private balcony overlooking Federation Square; request in‑room spa treatments for the ultimate indulgence.

Tip #3: Bespoke Culinary Tour with AR Tours

Enjoy a private chef’s table at Attica, followed by an exclusive, behind‑the‑scenes tour of the Royal Botanic Gardens’ secret garden.
