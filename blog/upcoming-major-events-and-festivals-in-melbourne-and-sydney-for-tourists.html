Live Update: Upcoming Major Events & Festivals in Melbourne & Sydney 2026

By AR Tours·September 20, 2026·5 min
Back to Blog

Tourists heading to Australia this year can look forward to a packed calendar of world‑class events. In Sydney, the Sydney Festival runs from 8 – 25 January 2026, while the Sydney Fringe World Festival stretches from 21 January to 15 February 2026. Melbourne’s cultural season kicks off with the Melbourne Food & Wine Festival in March, followed by the electronic music showcase RISING in June. Sports lovers can mark the Australian Motorcycle Grand Prix in late October and the iconic Melbourne Cup Carnival from late October into November.
