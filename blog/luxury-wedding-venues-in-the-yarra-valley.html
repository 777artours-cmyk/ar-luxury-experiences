Spotlight on Exquisite Yarra Valley Wedding Venues

Prepare to be captivated by these premier venues, each offering a distinct flavour of luxury for your wedding day.

Stones of the Yarra Valley: A Timeless Romance

  • Nestled amongst vines and aged oak trees, Stones of the Yarra Valley is perhaps the region's most iconic luxury wedding destination. It boasts a collection of beautifully restored buildings, including a charming chapel, a magnificent barn, and a sophisticated dairy, each exuding rustic elegance and timeless charm.
  • Couples can choose from multiple stunning ceremony and reception spaces, offering versatility and breathtaking photographic opportunities at every turn. The exceptional culinary team delivers exquisite seasonal menus, perfectly paired with local wines.
  • AR Tours provides discreet and luxurious transport for the bridal party and guests, ensuring a grand entrance and departure from this enchanting estate.

Zonzo Estate: Italian Charm Meets Vineyard Grandeur

  • Perched on a hilltop with panoramic views of the Yarra Valley, Zonzo Estate offers a breathtaking backdrop for a modern, sophisticated wedding. Its award-winning restaurant and unique barrel room provide a charming, rustic-luxe setting with an Italian influence.
  • The estate's dedicated event spaces, coupled with its renowned wood-fired pizzas and exquisite wine list, create a relaxed yet elegant atmosphere perfect for celebrating love. The sprawling grounds offer endless photo opportunities, from the vineyards to the olive groves.
  • Experience the journey to Zonzo in ultimate comfort and style with AR Tours' premium chauffeur service, allowing you and your guests to savour every moment without a worry.

Levantine Hill Estate: Modern Luxury Redefined

  • For those seeking a truly contemporary and ultra-luxurious experience, Levantine Hill Estate stands apart. This architectural masterpiece combines world-class winemaking with avant-garde design, offering a sophisticated and exclusive setting for your wedding.
  • Featuring striking modern interiors, a private dining room, and expansive outdoor spaces overlooking meticulously tended vineyards, Levantine Hill provides a unique blend of art, design, and gastronomy. Their award-winning restaurant and exceptional service promise an unparalleled celebration.
  • Arrive in unparalleled style with AR Tours, where our luxury fleet ensures your journey to this architectural marvel is as impressive as the destination itself.
