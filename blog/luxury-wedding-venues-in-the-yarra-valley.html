Luxury Wedding Venues in the Yarra Valley

The Yarra Valley, located just outside of Melbourne, Victoria, is a renowned destination for luxury weddings. With its picturesque landscapes, world-class wineries, and exquisite venues, it's the perfect place to tie the knot in style. At AR Tours, we've had the pleasure of chauffeuring countless couples to and from their dream wedding venues, and we're excited to share our expertise with you.

From intimate gatherings to grand affairs, the Yarra Valley has a venue to suit every taste and budget. Some of the most popular luxury wedding venues in the area include:

  • Chateau Yering: a historic mansion with stunning views of the Yarra Valley
  • Levantine Hill: a luxurious vineyard estate with breathtaking views of the surrounding countryside
  • Yering Station: a picturesque winery with beautiful gardens and a tranquil atmosphere

These venues offer a range of amenities and services, from gourmet catering to luxurious accommodations, to ensure that your special day is nothing short of perfection. And with AR Tours, you can trust that your transportation needs will be taken care of, with our fleet of luxurious vehicles and experienced chauffeurs.
