Iconic Destinations: The Pillars of Melbourne Art

Any comprehensive Melbourne art tour must include its most revered institutions. With AR Tours, you’ll experience these cultural landmarks in the most refined manner:

  • The National Gallery of Victoria (NGV)

    As Australia's oldest and most visited art gallery, the NGV is a dual marvel. NGV International, located on St Kilda Road, houses an extraordinary collection of international art, spanning ancient civilisations to contemporary global works. Its impressive stained-glass ceiling and diverse exhibitions make it a cornerstone of Melbourne's art scene. Just a short, comfortable drive away, in Federation Square, you'll find The Ian Potter Centre: NGV Australia. Dedicated entirely to Australian art, it presents a breathtaking journey through Indigenous art, colonial works, and modern Australian masterpieces. Your AR Tours chauffeur ensures smooth transitions between these two magnificent sites, maximising your time for appreciation.

  • The Australian Centre for Contemporary Art (ACCA)

    For those with a penchant for the avant-garde, ACCA is a must-visit. Recognisable by its striking rusted steel architecture, ACCA is a non-collecting gallery dedicated solely to commissioning, exhibiting, and promoting contemporary art. Its thought-provoking and often challenging exhibitions offer a window into the current trends and future directions of art, making it an exhilarating stop on your tour. With AR Tours, you arrive refreshed and ready to engage with the cutting edge.

  • Heide Museum of Modern Art

    A tranquil escape just outside the city centre, Heide is more than just a gallery; it's a place where art, architecture, and nature intertwine. Once the home of art patrons John and Sunday Reed, Heide was a hub for some of Australia's most significant modernist artists. Today, it features three exhibition spaces, a sculpture park, and beautiful gardens. Exploring Heide offers a unique blend of art history, natural beauty, and a poignant connection to Australian modernism. Your private chauffeur makes the scenic journey effortless, allowing you to fully absorb the serene atmosphere.
