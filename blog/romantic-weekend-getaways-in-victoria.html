Yarra Valley: A Toast to Love

Just an hour's drive from Melbourne, the Yarra Valley beckons with its undulating vineyards, gourmet delights, and serene beauty. This acclaimed wine region is perfect for couples who appreciate the finer things in life.

  • Wine Tasting & Dining: Indulge in world-class cellar door experiences at iconic wineries like Domaine Chandon or TarraWarra Estate. Many offer exquisite degustation menus paired with their award-winning cool-climate wines.
  • Hot Air Ballooning: For an unforgettable sunrise experience, float majestically above the valley, witnessing breathtaking panoramic views as the world awakens below.
  • Art & Gardens: Explore charming local art galleries and wander through stunning gardens, adding culture and beauty to your escape.

AR Tours provides seamless, luxurious transport, allowing you to savour every sip and moment without a single worry about driving.

Mornington Peninsula: Coastal Charm & Indulgence

South of Melbourne, the Mornington Peninsula offers a captivating blend of rugged coastline, pristine beaches, and sophisticated indulgence. It's a versatile haven for couples seeking both invigorating adventure and profound relaxation.

  • Peninsula Hot Springs: Immerse yourselves in natural thermal mineral waters, choosing from hilltop pools with 360-degree views or intimate, private bathing experiences.
  • Coastal Walks & Beaches: Stroll hand-in-hand along stunning beaches like Sorrento or Portsea, or explore dramatic clifftop walks and scenic coastal trails.
  • Gourmet Food Trail: Discover an abundance of farm gates, artisanal producers, and renowned restaurants serving incredibly fresh, local produce and exquisite seafood.

With AR Tours, your journey along the picturesque coastal roads is as comfortable as it is scenic, providing ample opportunity to simply relax and soak in the beauty together.

Daylesford & Hepburn Springs: The Spa Country Sanctuary

Nestled in the heart of Victoria's revered spa country, Daylesford and Hepburn Springs are synonymous with rejuvenation, tranquility, and holistic wellness. It’s the ultimate destination for couples looking to unwind, reconnect, and pamper themselves.

  • Mineral Spas & Wellness: Experience world-class spa treatments, therapeutic mineral baths, and soothing massages at renowned establishments like Hepburn Bathhouse & Spa or The Lake House.
  • Boutique Accommodation: Choose from charming cottages, luxury villas, or opulent hotels, many featuring private jacuzzis, crackling fireplaces, and stunning views.
  • Lakeside Strolls & Galleries: Enjoy leisurely, romantic walks around the picturesque Lake Daylesford, browse unique local art galleries, or discover hidden treasures in antique shops.

Let AR Tours whisk you away to this serene sanctuary, ensuring a peaceful, comfortable, and utterly pampered start to your romantic retreat.
