Yarra Valley: A Toast to Timeless Romance

Just an hour's scenic drive from Melbourne, the Yarra Valley unfurls as a picturesque haven for wine connoisseurs and lovers of fine living. Its rolling vineyards, framed by distant mountains, provide an idyllic backdrop for an unforgettable romantic escape.

  • Gourmet Indulgence: Embark on exclusive cellar door tastings at world-renowned wineries such as Domaine Chandon, Yering Station, or TarraWarra Estate. Savour award-winning Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, often accompanied by breathtaking views.
  • Culinary Excellence: Elevate your dining at hatted restaurants like Levantine Hill or Oakridge Wines, where exquisite regional cuisine is perfectly paired with local vintages.
  • Sky-High Serenity: For a truly unforgettable moment, consider a sunrise hot air balloon ride. Drifting gracefully above the mist-shrouded valleys as the sun paints the sky is pure magic, followed by a gourmet breakfast.

With AR Tours, your exploration of the Yarra Valley is luxurious and stress-free. Our private chauffeurs ensure you can fully immerse yourselves in every tasting and moment, without a single worry about navigation or designated drivers.

Mornington Peninsula: Coastal Elegance and Rejuvenation

For couples drawn to the allure of the sea, gourmet food, and blissful relaxation, the Mornington Peninsula is a jewel on Victoria's coastline, blending stunning natural beauty with sophisticated leisure.

  • Beachside Bliss: Stroll hand-in-hand along the pristine, sandy shores of Sorrento or Portsea, or discover hidden coves for intimate picnics.
  • Thermal Sanctuary: Immerse yourselves in the soothing mineral waters of Peninsula Hot Springs. Indulge in private bathing experiences or a couples massage, leaving you both feeling utterly refreshed.
  • Artisan Adventures: Explore charming seaside towns, browse bespoke boutiques, and discover a vibrant culinary scene. From fresh seafood to artisan cheese producers, the Peninsula offers a feast for all senses.

AR Tours provides a smooth, luxurious journey along the scenic coastal routes, allowing you to absorb the beauty without distraction.

Daylesford & Hepburn Springs: A Sanctuary of Spa and Serenity

Often hailed as Australia's spa capital, Daylesford and Hepburn Springs offer a unique blend of natural mineral springs, holistic wellness, and gourmet delights, ideal for couples seeking tranquility and rejuvenation.

  • Spa Indulgence: Unwind together with bespoke spa treatments, mineral water therapies, and couples massages at world-class retreats like The Lake House or Hepburn Bathhouse & Spa.
  • Charming Discoveries: Wander through quaint galleries, antique shops, and local markets, uncovering unique treasures and local artistry.
  • Gastronomic Delights: Dine at award-winning restaurants that champion local produce, offering intimate and memorable culinary experiences.
  • Nature's Embrace: Enjoy a romantic stroll around the serene Lake Daylesford or explore beautiful botanical gardens, soaking in the peaceful ambience.

The Dandenong Ranges: Enchanting Forest Escapes

For couples desiring a whimsical, fairytale-like escape, the Dandenong Ranges, just an hour east of Melbourne, offer a cool, green sanctuary.

  • Puffing Billy Romance: Take a nostalgic ride on the iconic Puffing Billy steam train, winding through lush fern gullies and towering eucalypts.
  • Garden of Eden: Explore the magnificent National Rhododendron Gardens or the Alfred Nicholas Memorial Gardens, where vibrant colours and serene water features create a magical setting.
  • High Tea Delights: Indulge in a traditional high tea at one of the charming guesthouses or cafes nestled amongst the trees.
