The Allure of Mornington Peninsula Hot Springs

The Mornington Peninsula Hot Springs is Australia’s first natural thermal mineral springs and spa destination. Drawing geothermal water from deep underground, rich in minerals, these springs offer a myriad of bathing experiences designed to soothe both body and mind. Whether you seek quiet contemplation or a vibrant social soak, the springs cater to every preference.

  • The Bath House: A labyrinth of pools, including a hill-top pool with panoramic 360-degree views of the Peninsula, cave pools, a Turkish hamam, saunas, and cold plunge pools. It's a vibrant space perfect for exploration and discovery.
  • The Spa Dreaming Centre: For those desiring a more serene and intimate experience, this adults-only sanctuary offers private bathing pavilions, a tranquil bathing gully, and a range of spa treatments designed to elevate your relaxation.
  • Wellness Retreat: Offering programs and experiences focused on holistic wellbeing, including yoga, Pilates, and mindful movement sessions, complementing your thermal journey.

Beyond the thermal waters, the Hot Springs embrace a philosophy of wellness, offering nourishing dining options, serene walking paths, and breathtaking views that truly transport you away from the everyday.
