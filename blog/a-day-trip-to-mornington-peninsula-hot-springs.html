The Enchantment of Mornington Peninsula Hot Springs

The Mornington Peninsula Hot Springs is a truly unique natural wonder, offering an extensive range of bathing experiences set amidst a beautiful, natural landscape. Sourced from 637 metres below ground, the natural thermal mineral waters flow into over 70 globally-inspired bathing experiences, each designed to soothe your body and calm your mind.

  • Hilltop Pool: Offering breathtaking 360-degree views of the Mornington Peninsula, this iconic pool is a must-experience.
  • Cave Pool: A secluded, atmospheric pool providing a unique and intimate bathing experience.
  • Reflexology Walk: Invigorate your senses and stimulate pressure points with this therapeutic walking experience.
  • Turkish Steam Bath (Hammam): Indulge in traditional cleansing and purifying rituals.
  • Saunas & Cold Plunge Pools: Enhance circulation and detoxify with contrasting hot and cold therapies.

Whether you choose to simply soak in the mineral-rich waters, enjoy a private bathing pavilion, or indulge in a spa treatment, the Hot Springs promises a profoundly restorative experience. The tranquil ambiance, coupled with the natural healing properties of the water, creates an idyllic setting for a perfect day out.

Crafting Your Perfect Peninsula Day: Beyond the Baths
