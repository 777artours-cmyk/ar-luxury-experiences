Private Great Ocean Road Tour vs Self-Drive: The Honest Comparison

By AR Tours·July 18, 2026·10-12 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction

The Great Ocean Road is one of Australia's most iconic destinations, stretching 243 kilometers along the southeastern coast of Victoria. With its stunning coastline, rock formations, and scenic views, it's a must-visit for anyone traveling to Melbourne. But when it comes to experiencing the Great Ocean Road, there are two main options: taking a private tour or self-driving. In this article, we'll compare the two and help you decide which is best for you.

Private Great Ocean Road Tour

A private Great Ocean Road tour offers a luxurious and personalized experience. With a private chauffeur and tour guide, you'll have the opportunity to learn about the history and significance of the area, while also enjoying the stunning views. Some of the benefits of a private tour include:

  • Personalized service and attention
  • Flexibility to create your own itinerary
  • Access to exclusive locations and experiences
  • No need to worry about driving or parking

Self-Drive Great Ocean Road Tour

On the other hand, self-driving the Great Ocean Road can be a fun and adventurous experience. With the freedom to stop and go as you please, you can take your time to enjoy the views and explore the area. Some of the benefits of self-driving include:

  • Flexibility to create your own itinerary
  • Ability to stop and go as you please
  • Cost-effective option
  • Opportunity to explore hidden gems and secret spots

However, self-driving also requires a lot of planning and research, and can be tiring and stressful, especially if you're not used to driving in Australia. Additionally, you'll need to consider the cost of fuel, tolls, and parking, which can add up quickly.
