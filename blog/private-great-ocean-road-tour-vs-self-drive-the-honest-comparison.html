Private Great Ocean Road Tour vs Self-Drive: The Honest Comparison

By AR Tours·August 3, 2026·10 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction

The Great Ocean Road is one of Australia's most iconic destinations, known for its breathtaking scenery and diverse wildlife. When planning a trip to this stunning region, one of the most important decisions is whether to take a private tour or self-drive. In this article, we will explore the benefits of each option and help you decide which is best for you.

Private Great Ocean Road Tour

A private Great Ocean Road tour with AR Tours offers a unique and personalized experience. Our expert guides will take you to hidden gems and share their knowledge of the region's history, geology, and wildlife. Some of the benefits of a private tour include:

  • Personalized service and flexibility
  • Expert guides with extensive knowledge of the region
  • Access to exclusive locations and experiences

Self-Drive

Self-driving the Great Ocean Road can be a fun and adventurous experience, allowing you to stop and explore at your own pace. However, it also requires a significant amount of planning and research to ensure you don't miss any of the highlights. Some of the benefits of self-driving include:

  • Flexibility to create your own itinerary
  • Freedom to stop and explore at your own pace
  • Cost-effective option for budget-conscious travelers
