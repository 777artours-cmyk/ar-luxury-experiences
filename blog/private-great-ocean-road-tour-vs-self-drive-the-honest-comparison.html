Private Great Ocean Road Tour vs Self-Drive: The Honest Comparison

By AR Tours·July 18, 2026·10-12 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction to the Great Ocean Road

The Great Ocean Road is one of Australia's most iconic and breathtaking destinations, located in the southeastern state of Victoria. Stretching over 243 kilometers, this stunning coastal route offers an unforgettable experience, with towering limestone cliffs, pristine beaches, and lush rainforests.

Private Great Ocean Road Tour

A private Great Ocean Road tour offers a unique and personalized experience. With a professional chauffeur and guide, you'll have the opportunity to explore the region's hidden gems, learn about its rich history, and enjoy the scenic views without the hassle of driving.

  • Personalized experience with a professional guide
  • Flexibility to create a customized itinerary
  • Stress-free travel without the need to drive

Self-Drive Option

On the other hand, a self-drive option provides the freedom to explore the Great Ocean Road at your own pace. With a rental car, you can stop whenever you want, take in the views, and enjoy the scenic routes without being tied to a specific schedule.

  • Flexibility to create your own itinerary
  • Opportunity to stop and explore at your own pace
  • Potential cost savings compared to a private tour

However, a self-drive option also means that you'll be responsible for navigating the roads, finding parking, and dealing with potential traffic congestion. Additionally, you may miss out on the valuable insights and knowledge that a professional guide can provide.

For a hassle-free and unforgettable experience, consider booking a private Great Ocean Road tour with AR Tours.

Conclusion

Ultimately, the choice between a private Great Ocean Road tour and a self-drive option depends on your personal preferences and priorities. If you value a stress-free and personalized experience, a private tour may be the better choice. However, if you prefer the freedom to explore at your own pace, a self-drive option may be more suitable.

Whichever option you choose, make sure to take in the breathtaking views, learn about the region's rich history, and enjoy the unforgettable experience that the Great Ocean Road has to offer.
