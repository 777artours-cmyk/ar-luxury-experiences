Introduction to Cape Otway Lighthouse

The Cape Otway Lighthouse is a must-visit attraction on the Great Ocean Road. This historic lighthouse is home to a variety of local wildlife, including rare birds.

Wildlife and Birdlife

Some of the wildlife you can spot at Cape Otway Lighthouse includes:

  • Koalas
  • Echidnas
  • Native birds
