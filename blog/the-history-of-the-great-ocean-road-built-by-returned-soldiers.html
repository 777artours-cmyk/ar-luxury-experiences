The History of the Great Ocean Road: Built by Returned Soldiers

By AR Tours·July 6, 2026·10 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction to the Great Ocean Road

The Great Ocean Road is a 243-kilometer stretch of road that winds its way along the southeastern coast of Australia, in the state of Victoria. It is one of the most iconic and scenic drives in the world, attracting millions of visitors each year. But the history of the Great Ocean Road is just as fascinating as its stunning natural beauty.

The Construction of the Great Ocean Road

The Great Ocean Road was built by returned soldiers between 1919 and 1932, as a way to provide employment and honor the soldiers who had fought in World War I. The construction of the road was a massive undertaking, with over 3,000 workers involved in the project. The road was built using pickaxes, shovels, and explosives, and it took over 13 years to complete.

The returned soldiers who built the Great Ocean Road faced many challenges, including harsh weather conditions, steep terrain, and limited resources. Despite these challenges, they persevered and created a road that would become one of Australia's most beloved tourist attractions.

Significance of the Great Ocean Road

The Great Ocean Road is significant not only for its natural beauty but also for its historical and cultural importance. It is a symbol of the sacrifice and bravery of the soldiers who built it, and it continues to be an important part of Australian identity. The road has also played a significant role in the development of Victoria's tourism industry, with millions of visitors each year contributing to the local economy.

Some of the key features of the Great Ocean Road include:

  • The Twelve Apostles, a series of limestone stacks that rise out of the ocean
  • The London Bridge rock formation, which was damaged in 1990 but still remains a popular tourist attraction
  • The Great Otway National Park, which is home to a diverse range of flora and fauna
  • The surf beaches and coastal towns, such as Torquay and Apollo Bay, which offer a range of accommodation and activities for visitors

Visiting the Great Ocean Road

Today, the Great Ocean Road is one of Australia's most popular tourist attractions, with visitors from all over the world coming to experience its natural beauty and rich history.
