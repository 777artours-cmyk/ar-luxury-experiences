Great Ocean Road in Winter: Why the Off-Season Is Actually Perfect

By AR Tours·July 16, 2026·10 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction to the Great Ocean Road

The Great Ocean Road is one of Australia's most iconic destinations, stretching over 243 kilometers along the southeastern coast of Victoria. While it's a popular spot during the summer months, the winter season offers a unique and unforgettable experience. As a premier private chauffeur and tour company in Melbourne, AR Tours is here to guide you through the benefits of visiting the Great Ocean Road in winter.

Less Crowds, More Intimacy

One of the main advantages of visiting the Great Ocean Road during the off-season is the significantly reduced number of tourists. This means you'll have a more intimate and personalized experience, with fewer people to contend with at popular attractions like the Twelve Apostles and Loch Ard Gorge. You'll have ample opportunity to take in the breathtaking scenery, snap photos, and soak up the atmosphere without the hustle and bustle of peak season.

Unique Perspectives and Landscapes

The winter season brings a fresh perspective to the Great Ocean Road, with the rugged coastline and surrounding landscapes taking on a dramatic and moody tone. The cooler weather also makes for a more comfortable and enjoyable walking experience, allowing you to explore the region's many trails and lookouts without the heat and humidity of summer. Some of the top attractions and activities to enjoy during winter include:

  • Witnessing the majestic sight of whales migrating along the coast
  • Exploring the beautiful waterfalls and rainforests of the Otway Ranges
  • Taking a scenic drive along the Great Ocean Road, with the misty rainforests and rugged coastline creating a breathtaking backdrop
  • Visiting the charming seaside towns, such as Apollo Bay and Lorne, with their cozy cafes and boutique shops
