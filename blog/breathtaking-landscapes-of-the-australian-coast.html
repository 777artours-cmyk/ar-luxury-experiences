Breathtaking Landscapes of the Australian Coast: A Luxury Traveller’s Guide

By AR Tours·October 14, 2026·7
Back to Blog

Introduction

Australia’s coastline stretches over 25,000 km, offering a kaleidoscope of cliffs, beaches, and marine wonders. For the discerning traveller, AR Tours curates experiences that blend luxury with the raw beauty of nature.

Iconic Coastal Highlights

Great Ocean Road, Victoria – A UNESCO World Heritage drive famed for the Twelve Apostles, towering limestone stacks that plunge into the Southern Ocean. Arrive at sunrise for a private yacht charter that glides beneath the cliffs.

Whitsunday Islands, Queensland – Home to Whitehaven Beach’s silica‑white sands and crystal‑clear waters. Stay at a boutique resort on Hamilton Island and enjoy a seaplane tour over the reef.

Hidden Gems

The Coral Coast, Western Australia – Beyond the popular Ningaloo Reef, the remote Cape Range offers rugged gorges and secluded bays where you can snorkel with manta rays away from the crowds.

Byron Bay’s Hinterland, New South Wales – Venture inland to the Redberry Falls and the ancient rainforest of Nightcap National Park, a short drive from the famed surf town.
