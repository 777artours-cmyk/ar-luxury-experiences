Why You Need a Private Guide on the Great Ocean Road

By AR Tours·November 7, 2026·7
Back to Blog

The Great Ocean Road is more than a postcard‑perfect drive; it’s a living museum of geology, Aboriginal heritage, and coastal culture. While the 243‑kilometre ribbon of asphalt offers breathtaking vistas at every turn, a private guide from AR Tours ensures you experience the road’s depth, not just its beauty.

Unlock Hidden Attractions

From the towering limestone stacks of the Twelve Apices to the secluded Grotto near Port Campbell, a guide knows the exact pull‑outs and timing to avoid crowds. You’ll also explore the lesser‑known Bay of Islands and the hidden waterfall at Erskine Falls, spots often missed by self‑drive tourists.

Rich History, Personal Storytelling

Built by returned soldiers after World I, the road is a tribute to resilience. A private guide weaves personal anecdotes, Aboriginal Dreamtime stories, and wartime anecdotes into each stop, turning a simple stop‑over into a narrative journey.

Tailored Experiences

Whether you crave a photography session at the iconic Twelve Apices at sunrise, a gourmet picnic at Gunnamatta Beach, or a marine‑wildlife briefing on whale‑watching season, a guide customises the itinerary on the fly.
