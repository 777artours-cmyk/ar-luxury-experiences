Ultimate Yarra Valley Wine Tour Guide 2026

From world-class Pinot Noir to sparkling wine perfection, discover the best wineries, cellar doors, and tasting experiences in Victoria's premier wine region.

Just an hour's drive east of Melbourne, the Yarra Valley is one of Australia's oldest and most celebrated wine regions. Established in 1838 when the Ryrie brothers planted the first vines at Yering Station, the valley has grown to encompass over 160 wineries, 80 cellar doors, and some of the finest cool-climate wines produced anywhere in the world.

The Yarra Valley's cool climate and diverse terroir make it particularly renowned for Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and sparkling wine. But that barely scratches the surface — you'll also find exceptional Shiraz, Cabernet Sauvignon, and some stunning Nebbiolo and Arneis from more adventurous producers. Whether you're a wine novice or a seasoned oenophile, our luxury Yarra Valley wine tour promises an unforgettable day of discovery.

The Must-Visit Wineries

🥂 Domaine Chandon

Australia's most prestigious sparkling wine house, Domaine Chandon is the Australian outpost of the French Champagne powerhouse Moët & Chandon. Nestled at the base of the Great Dividing Range, the winery's stunning modernist architecture features floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the vines and the distant mountains.

Their signature Vintage Brut and Blanc de Blancs are world-class, rivalling many genuine Champagnes. The tasting room offers guided experiences that walk you through the traditional method of sparkling wine production, from first pressing to riddling and disgorgement. The on-site restaurant, Raidis, serves a seasonal menu designed to pair perfectly with their wines — the degustation lunch is a genuine culinary highlight of the valley.

🎨 TarraWarra Estate

TarraWarra Estate is where wine meets art in spectacular fashion. Founded by Marc and Eva Besen in 1983, the estate is home to both a premium winery and the TarraWarra Museum of Art — a striking underground gallery designed by architect Allan Powell that hosts world-class contemporary art exhibitions throughout the year.

The wines are equally impressive. TarraWarra's single-vineyard Pinot Noir is consistently rated among Australia's best, with layers of red cherry, forest floor, and silky tannins. Their Chardonnay shows remarkable restraint and elegance. After your tasting, step into the museum for a completely unexpected cultural experience in the middle of wine country.

🏛️ Yering Station

As the site of Victoria's first vineyard, Yering Station holds a special place in Australian wine history. The estate combines a beautifully restored 1859 homestead with a striking modern winery and tasting room. Their wines span from the accessible Village range to the premium Reserve wines, offering something for every palate and budget.

The Reserve Shiraz Viognier — a Rhône-style blend — is exceptional and regularly collects gold medals. The estate's restaurant offers one of the valley's finest dining experiences, with views across the vines to the distant ranges. On weekends, their artisan farmers' market (third Sunday of each month) is a bustling celebration of local produce.

🌿 Oakridge Wines

If you want to understand what makes Yarra Valley wine truly special, Oakridge is the place to go. Chief winemaker David Bicknell is renowned for his single-vineyard, site-specific approach — each wine reflects the exact soil, aspect, and microclimate where its grapes were grown.

Their 864 Chardonnay and 864 Pinot Noir (named for the vineyard's elevation in feet above sea level) are extraordinary examples of what cool-climate winemaking can achieve. The cellar door offers an intimate Winemaker's Table experience, where you taste through the vineyard-specific range with expert commentary on the terroir that shapes each wine.

🍷 De Bortoli

One of Australia's great family wine dynasties, De Bortoli in the Yarra Valley focuses on premium cool-climate wines that are a world apart from their Riverina roots. The estate's centrepiece is Locale, a casual Italian-inspired restaurant that celebrates the connection between wine and food in the most approachable way possible.

Their Section A5 Syrah showcases a peppery, elegant expression of the variety that's uniquely Yarra Valley. The cellar door team are wonderfully knowledgeable and never pretentious — perfect for visitors who want to learn without feeling intimidated.

🌅 Rochford Wines

Rochford offers one of the most striking settings in the valley, with a panoramic amphitheatre that hosts the famous Day on the Green concert series. The wines — particularly their Latitude Pinot Noir — are excellent, and the cellar door experience includes a fascinating journey through their barrel room.

The on-site restaurant serves contemporary Australian cuisine with views that stretch across the valley. It's an ideal spot for a leisurely lunch between tastings.

Beyond the Wineries

Yarra Valley Chocolaterie & Ice Creamery

No Yarra Valley tour is complete without a stop at the Yarra Valley Chocolaterie. This Belgian-style chocolate factory offers free tastings of over 250 varieties of handmade chocolate. Watch the chocolatiers at work through viewing windows, then indulge in their legendary hot chocolate — made from real melted chocolate, not cocoa powder. The ice creamery next door produces small-batch gelato in flavours that change seasonally.

Healesville Sanctuary

Run by Zoos Victoria, Healesville Sanctuary is one of the best places in Australia to see native wildlife up close. Set in 30 hectares of natural bushland, the sanctuary is home to platypus, Tasmanian devils, wombats, echidnas, and a spectacular birds of prey show. The keeper talks are fascinating and the sanctuary's conservation breeding programs for endangered species are genuinely world-leading.

Four Pillars Gin Distillery

The Yarra Valley isn't just about wine. Four Pillars, named "International Gin Producer of the Year" multiple times, operates a stunning distillery and bar in Healesville. Their Rare Dry Gin is considered one of the world's finest, and their seasonal releases — including the famous Bloody Shiraz Gin made by steeping Yarra Valley Shiraz grapes in gin — are collector's items.

🍇 Best Time to Visit

Autumn (March-May) is magical — golden vine leaves, harvest activity, and new vintage releases. Spring (September-November) offers wildflowers and crisp wines. Winter brings cosy fireside tastings. Summer (December-February) is peak season with long days, outdoor dining, and festivals — book well ahead.

Planning Your Wine Tour

The biggest mistake visitors make is trying to cram in too many wineries. We recommend visiting 3-4 cellar doors in a day, with a proper sit-down lunch at one of them. This allows time to savour each tasting, chat with the winemakers, and actually enjoy the stunning landscapes between stops.

Driving yourself means one person misses out on the wine. A private wine tour with AR Tours solves this beautifully — everyone can taste freely while your experienced guide and driver navigates the valley's winding roads. Our guides have deep relationships with the valley's winemakers and can often arrange private barrel tastings and behind-the-scenes experiences not available to the general public.
