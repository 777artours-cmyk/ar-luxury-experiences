The Majestic Great Ocean Road: Coastal Wonders for All Ages

The Great Ocean Road is a world-renowned spectacle, and while the full journey might be ambitious for a single day with young children, key sections offer breathtaking beauty and family fun. Start your adventure with AR Tours, focusing on the eastern stretch, where you can marvel at the iconic Split Point Lighthouse at Aireys Inlet, explore the charming seaside towns of Lorne and Apollo Bay, and enjoy pristine beaches perfect for a quick paddle or a sandcastle-building competition. Our chauffeurs ensure a smooth, scenic drive, allowing your family to soak in the dramatic coastal views without distraction.

  • Split Point Lighthouse: Climb to the top for panoramic ocean views.
  • Lorne & Apollo Bay: Relax on the beach, grab an ice cream, or enjoy a casual lunch.
  • Erskine Falls: A short, accessible walk to a beautiful waterfall (check accessibility for prams if needed).

For those eager to experience more of this iconic route, AR Tours offers dedicated Great Ocean Road tours, ensuring a comprehensive yet comfortable exploration.

Phillip Island: A Wildlife Wonderland

A visit to Phillip Island is a quintessential Melbourne family experience, culminating in the magical Penguin Parade. AR Tours ensures your journey is as enchanting as the destination itself. Our private chauffeurs will transport you comfortably to the island, allowing ample time to explore before the main event.

  • Penguin Parade: Witness hundreds of little penguins waddle ashore at sunset – an unforgettable spectacle.
  • Koala Conservation Centre: Get up close with koalas in their natural habitat from tree-top boardwalks.
  • Churchill Island Heritage Farm: Step back in time and meet farm animals, perfect for younger children.
  • The Nobbies Centre: Explore boardwalks offering stunning coastal views and spot seals.

With AR Tours, you won't have to worry about the late-night drive back to Melbourne after the Penguin Parade; simply relax and recount the day's adventures.

Yarra Valley: Nature, Noms, and Native Animals

While famous for its wineries, the Yarra Valley offers a wealth of family-friendly attractions that go beyond the cellar door. It's an ideal destination for a relaxing day trip, blending nature, delicious treats, and wildlife encounters.

  • Healesville Sanctuary: A must-visit to see Australia's unique wildlife, including koalas, kangaroos, and platypuses, in a beautiful bushland setting. Interactive shows and keeper talks engage children and adults alike.
  • Yarra Valley Chocolaterie & Ice Creamery: Indulge in free chocolate tastings, watch chocolatiers at work, and let the kids run wild in the expansive gardens. It's a sweet stop guaranteed to delight.
  • Pony Rides & Berry Picking: Depending on the season, several local farms offer delightful activities for children.

AR Tours can arrange a seamless journey through the picturesque Yarra Valley, allowing your family to savour every moment without a single logistical concern.

Dandenong Ranges: Puffing Billy and Enchanting Forests

Escape to the cool, green embrace of the Dandenong Ranges, a tranquil retreat just an hour's drive from Melbourne. This region offers a charming mix of natural beauty and nostalgic experiences, perfect for families.

  • Puffing Billy Steam Train: Take a ride on Australia's oldest and best-preserved steam railway. Dangle your legs out the carriage windows as you chug through lush fern gullies and towering forests – a truly unique and memorable experience for all ages.
  • Emerald Lake Park: Enjoy a picnic, paddle a boat on the lake, or let the kids play in the adventure playground.
  • William Ricketts Sanctuary: Discover fascinating hand-carved sculptures set amidst a serene rainforest.

With AR Tours, your journey to the Dandenongs is as picturesque as the destination itself, ensuring a comfortable and stress-free ascent into this magical mountain range.
