Wildlife You Can Spot Along the Great Ocean Road: Koalas, Kangaroos and More

By AR Tours·July 4, 2026·10 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction to the Great Ocean Road

The Great Ocean Road is one of Victoria's most iconic tourist destinations, stretching over 243 kilometers along the southeastern coast of Australia. This stunning scenic drive is not only known for its breathtaking ocean views, limestone cliffs, and beautiful beaches but also for its diverse and unique wildlife.

Koalas: The Iconic Australian Marsupial

Koalas are one of the most recognizable and beloved Australian animals. These cute and cuddly creatures can be found in the eucalyptus forests and woodlands along the Great Ocean Road. The best places to spot koalas are at the Kennett River, where they can often be seen lazing around in the trees, and at the Great Otway National Park, which is home to a large and healthy koala population.

Kangaroos and Wallabies: Hopping Across the Landscape

Kangaroos and wallabies are another iconic Australian animal that can be found in the wild along the Great Ocean Road. These amazing creatures can often be seen hopping across the landscape, particularly at dawn and dusk when they are most active. Some of the best places to spot kangaroos and wallabies include the Great Otway National Park, the Anglesea Heath, and the coastal areas around Torquay.

Some of the other wildlife you can spot along the Great Ocean Road includes:

  • Echidnas: small, spiny mammals that can be found in the undergrowth and forests
  • Platypus: one of the most unique and fascinating creatures in the animal kingdom, which can be found in the rivers and streams
  • Seals and sea lions: which can often be seen basking in the sun on the rocks and beaches
  • Whales: which can be seen migrating along the coast during the winter months
  • Over 200 species of birds: including parrots, eagles, and honeyeaters
