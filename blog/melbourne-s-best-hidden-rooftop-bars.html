Why Melbourne's Rooftop Bars Are a Must-Experience

Melbourne's rooftop bar scene is unparalleled, offering a diverse range of atmospheres from intimate and quirky to sleek and sophisticated. What makes them truly special is their often-hidden nature – tucked away atop unassuming buildings, accessed via discreet elevators, or found at the end of an unexpected laneway. This element of discovery adds to their allure, making each visit feel like uncovering a secret. Beyond the novelty, these establishments often boast innovative cocktail menus, gourmet bites, and panoramic vistas that offer a fresh perspective on the city's iconic skyline.

Our Top Picks for Melbourne's Hidden Rooftop Bars

  • Goldilocks Bar: The Intimate Escape

    Perched above the bustling streets of Swanston Street, Goldilocks Bar is a true hidden gem. Accessed through a non-descript door and up an elevator, this intimate space feels like a well-kept secret. Its compact size contributes to its cozy, exclusive atmosphere, making it perfect for a quiet, sophisticated drink. The décor is understated yet stylish, allowing the stunning views of the State Library and city skyline to take centre stage. Their cocktail menu is thoughtfully curated, often featuring seasonal ingredients and creative twists on classics. It’s the ideal spot for an elegant pre-dinner drink or a romantic evening.

  • Easey's: The Quirky Urban Oasis

    For something truly unique and undeniably Melbourne, Easey's in Collingwood offers an experience like no other. This isn't just a rooftop bar; it's a multi-level venue featuring actual train carriages repurposed into dining and drinking spaces, all atop a building. The rooftop section, with its graffiti-clad walls and industrial charm, provides an edgy, authentic Melbourne vibe. Famous for its burgers and local craft beers, Easey's combines quirky aesthetics with incredible views of the city. It's a testament to Melbourne's creative spirit and a must-visit for those seeking an unconventional, fun-loving rooftop adventure.

  • Siglo Bar: European Elegance Above the City

    Above the iconic European Bier Café and Supper Club on Spring Street, Siglo Bar offers a timeless, sophisticated retreat. With its European-inspired ambiance, complete with classic outdoor furniture and elegant lighting, Siglo exudes old-world charm. It provides a breathtaking, uninterrupted view of Parliament House, St. Patrick's Cathedral, and the city lights, making it an incredibly picturesque spot. Renowned for its extensive wine list, premium spirits, and selection of fine cigars, Siglo is the epitome of luxurious relaxation. It's the perfect setting for a sophisticated evening, where conversations flow as smoothly as the champagne.

  • Loop Roof & Loop Project Space: Art, Views, and Cocktails

    Tucked away above Meyers Place, Loop Roof offers a vibrant and ever-evolving experience. This multi-level venue combines a cutting-edge art gallery (Loop Project Space) on the lower floors with a dynamic rooftop bar. The rooftop itself is a visually stimulating space, adorned with lush greenery, unique art installations, and often featuring projections or video art. It boasts spectacular 360-degree views of the city, making it a fantastic spot to watch the sunset transform the skyline. Their cocktail menu is as creative as their surroundings, offering a range of innovative concoctions alongside classic favourites. Loop Roof is perfect for those who appreciate art and a lively, chic atmosphere.
