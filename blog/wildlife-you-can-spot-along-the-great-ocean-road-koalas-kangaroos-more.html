Discover the Wild Heart of the Great Ocean Road with AR Tours

The Great Ocean Road isn't just a scenic drive; it's a living, breathing canvas where Australia's iconic wildlife thrives amidst some of the world's most spectacular coastal scenery. Koalas: The Great Ocean Road's Beloved Icon

No visit to the Great Ocean Road is complete without a sighting of Australia's most beloved marsupial: the koala. While they can be elusive, our expert chauffeurs know the prime locations where these adorable creatures are often found. The tranquil eucalyptus forests around Kennett River and Cape Otway are renowned koala hotspots. Here, you'll often see them perched high in the gum trees, munching on leaves or simply enjoying a long nap. AR Tours ensures you have ample time and the best vantage points for photography, allowing you to observe these gentle creatures in their natural habitat without disturbing them. Our private tours offer the flexibility to linger and truly appreciate these moments, far from crowded tour buses.

Kangaroos & Wallabies: Majestic Marsupials of the Bush and Coast

As you journey further along the Great Ocean Road, particularly in the open fields and golf courses near Anglesea and Tower Hill, you'll likely encounter Australia's most famous jumper: the kangaroo. These majestic marsupials, often seen in "mobs" (groups), are most active during dawn and dusk, grazing peacefully as the sun rises or sets. Wallabies, smaller cousins of the kangaroo, are also prevalent, often preferring denser bushland. Our AR Tours chauffeurs are adept at spotting these magnificent animals, guiding you to areas where you can witness them in their natural environment, ensuring a respectful and memorable encounter. The serene beauty of watching kangaroos against the backdrop of the Australian bush is an experience that truly defines a luxury tour.

Echidnas: The Spiny Anteaters of the Forest Floor

Keep a keen eye out for one of Australia's most unique mammals, the echidna. These fascinating spiny anteaters are monotremes, meaning they lay eggs, much like the platypus. While more shy and harder to spot, they can occasionally be seen foraging for ants and termites on the forest floor, particularly in the heathlands and bush areas along the Great Ocean Road. Their distinctive waddle and sharp spines make them an unforgettable sight for those lucky enough to spot them. Your AR Tours private chauffeur will be on the lookout, enhancing your chances of encountering this extraordinary creature.

A Symphony of Feathers: Native Birdlife

The Great Ocean Road is a birdwatcher's paradise, boasting an incredible array of native bird species. From the raucous laughter of kookaburras echoing through the trees to the vibrant plumage of crimson rosellas and sulphur-crested cockatoos, the skies and forests are alive with colour and sound. You might spot majestic wedge-tailed eagles soaring high above the cliffs or tiny, iridescent fairy-wrens flitting through the undergrowth. At places like Maits Rest Rainforest Walk, the dense canopy provides a perfect habitat for a variety of species. AR Tours provides a tranquil setting, allowing you to immerse yourself in the natural symphony of Australia's diverse avian population.

Marine Wonders: Whales, Dolphins & Seals

Beyond the land, the pristine waters of the Great Ocean Road also host a wealth of marine life. During the winter months (May to September), the region becomes a prime viewing spot for migrating southern right whales and humpback whales, particularly around Logan's Beach Whale Nursery in Warrnambool. Throughout the year, pods of playful dolphins can often be seen surfing the waves, and Australian fur seals bask on rocky outcrops near Apollo Bay. Your AR Tours journey will include stops at key lookouts, offering panoramic views and the chance to spot these incredible ocean dwellers, adding another layer of wonder to your wildlife adventure.
