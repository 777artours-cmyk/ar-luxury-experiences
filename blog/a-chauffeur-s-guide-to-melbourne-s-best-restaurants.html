A Chauffeur's Curated Selection: Melbourne's Gastronomic Icons

From avant-garde tasting menus to classic European elegance, Melbourne's top restaurants offer an extraordinary spectrum of experiences. Here are some of the destinations our chauffeurs frequently recommend and expertly navigate:

  • Attica: The Avant-Garde Experience

    Located in Ripponlea, Attica is not just a restaurant; it’s a culinary journey. Chef Ben Shewry’s approach to Australian cuisine is revolutionary, utilising indigenous ingredients and storytelling to create a truly unique and often surprising tasting menu. A global icon, securing a table here is a feat in itself, and arriving in an AR Tours luxury vehicle ensures your grand entrance matches the occasion. It's an experience that transcends dining.

  • Vue de Monde: Dining Above the Clouds

    Perched on the 55th floor of the Rialto Tower, Vue de Monde offers breathtaking panoramic views of Melbourne. Under the guidance of Shannon Bennett, this restaurant delivers a theatrical and sophisticated fine dining experience with a focus on modern Australian cuisine. The journey to the top is part of the allure, and an AR Tours chauffeur ensures your ascent is as smooth and stylish as the descent, making for an unforgettable evening.

  • Gimlet at Cavendish House: Timeless Elegance

    Housed within the stunning heritage-listed Cavendish House, Gimlet is Andrew McConnell’s ode to classic European supper clubs. With its grand, art deco-inspired interiors and a menu that celebrates wood-fired cooking and impeccable produce, Gimlet exudes an undeniable charm and sophistication. It’s the perfect setting for a special occasion or a refined evening out, and our chauffeurs know exactly how to deliver you to its elegant doors with grace and ease.

  • Cutler & Co.: Modern Australian Brilliance in Fitzroy

    Also from the acclaimed Andrew McConnell, Cutler & Co. in Fitzroy offers a more relaxed yet equally refined take on modern Australian dining. Set in a converted industrial space, it combines a vibrant atmosphere with a menu driven by seasonal, high-quality ingredients. It’s a testament to Melbourne’s ability to blend casual sophistication with culinary excellence. Our chauffeurs can whisk you away to this vibrant locale, allowing you to immerse yourself in Fitzroy's unique energy.

  • Ishizuka: An Omakase Masterpiece

    For an intimate and truly exclusive Japanese dining experience, Ishizuka in the city's CBD offers an exquisite 16-course omakase menu. With only 16 seats, this subterranean restaurant provides a meticulous and artful culinary performance that showcases the finest seasonal ingredients. It’s a sanctuary of gastronomic precision, and an AR Tours chauffeur can ensure your arrival and departure are as seamless and discreet as the experience itself.
