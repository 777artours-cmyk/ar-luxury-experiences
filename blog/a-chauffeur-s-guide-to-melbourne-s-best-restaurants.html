Curated Culinary Destinations: A Chauffeur's Top Picks

Drawing on years of experience and countless journeys through Melbourne's dining precincts, our chauffeurs have developed an intimate knowledge of the city's premier restaurants. Here are a few of our top recommendations, where an AR Tours chauffeur can truly elevate your visit:

  • Vue de Monde: Perched atop the Rialto Tower, Vue de Monde offers an extraordinary modern Australian degustation experience with breathtaking panoramic views. Arriving in a luxurious AR Tours vehicle sets the perfect tone for an evening of sophisticated indulgence. Let us handle the ascent to this iconic establishment, ensuring your focus remains solely on the culinary artistry.
  • Attica: Consistently ranked among the world's best, Attica in Ripponlea is a pilgrimage for serious foodies. Chef Ben Shewry's innovative approach to Australian ingredients creates a truly unique and memorable dining experience. Given its suburban location, an AR Tours chauffeur ensures a relaxed and timely arrival, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in Attica's celebrated journey without any travel concerns.
  • Gimlet at Cavendish House: Housed in a stunning heritage building, Gimlet offers a sophisticated European-inspired menu in an exquisitely designed setting. It's the epitome of timeless elegance. An evening at Gimlet calls for an arrival in style, and your AR Tours chauffeur provides just that, delivering you to the door of this Flinders Lane gem with understated grace.
  • Flower Drum: A Melbourne institution, Flower Drum sets the gold standard for Cantonese fine dining. Renowned for its impeccable service and exquisite dishes, it promises an unparalleled experience. For a discreet and luxurious evening, an AR Tours chauffeur ensures your journey to and from this Spring Street icon is as refined as the meal itself.
  • Di Stasio Città: A vibrant and artful Italian restaurant in the heart of the city, Di Stasio Città is known for its bold flavours, stylish ambiance, and impressive art collection. Perfect for a pre-theatre dinner or a lively evening out. Let AR Tours transport you effortlessly to this dynamic culinary hotspot, allowing you to savour every moment, from aperitivo to digestivo.
