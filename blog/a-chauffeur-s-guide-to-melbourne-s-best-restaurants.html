The Pinnacle of Palates: Melbourne's Premier Dining Destinations

Allow us to guide you through some of Melbourne's most esteemed dining establishments, handpicked for their exceptional cuisine, ambiance, and the overall experience they offer. With AR Tours, every culinary adventure begins and ends in luxury.

  • Attica: A Journey into Australian Innovation

    Helmed by the visionary Ben Shewry, Attica is not merely a restaurant; it’s an immersive experience that redefines modern Australian cuisine. Located in Ripponlea, a short, comfortable drive from the city, Attica champions indigenous ingredients and sustainable practices, presenting dishes that are both thought-provoking and deeply delicious. Securing a reservation here is a feat in itself, but arriving in the comfort and style of an AR Tours chauffeur ensures your evening starts and ends on a note of effortless sophistication. Let us handle the logistics while you prepare for a culinary narrative unlike any other.

  • Vue de Monde: Dining Above the Clouds

    Perched on the 55th floor of the Rialto building, Vue de Monde offers breathtaking panoramic views of Melbourne, perfectly complementing its avant-garde Modern Australian degustation menu. Shannon Bennett's iconic establishment is synonymous with luxury, theatrical presentation, and impeccable service. The journey to this sky-high sanctuary is part of the experience, and with AR Tours, you’ll be whisked away to the heart of the city, arriving directly at the entrance, ready to ascend to your extraordinary dining experience without a single worry about parking or navigation.

  • Gimlet at Cavendish House: Timeless Elegance

    Andrew McConnell’s Gimlet at Cavendish House brings a touch of old-world European charm and sophisticated dining to Melbourne’s CBD. Housed in a beautifully restored 1920s building, Gimlet exudes timeless elegance with its stunning interiors, classic cocktail bar, and menu featuring wood-fired dishes and exceptional seafood. It’s the perfect spot for a romantic evening or a distinguished business dinner. An AR Tours chauffeur will ensure your arrival is as polished as the venue itself, allowing you to immerse yourself fully in its captivating ambiance from the moment you step out of our luxury vehicle.

  • Cutler & Co.: Modern Australian Refinement

    Another jewel in Andrew McConnell's crown, Cutler & Co. in Fitzroy offers a refined yet relaxed modern Australian dining experience. Known for its sophisticated menu that celebrates seasonal produce, an extensive wine list, and an industrial-chic setting, it's a favourite among discerning locals. Navigating Fitzroy's vibrant streets can be a challenge, but with AR Tours, your journey to Cutler & Co. will be smooth and stress-free, allowing you to anticipate the culinary delights that await.

  • Flower Drum: The Epitome of Cantonese Luxury

    For those seeking an unparalleled Cantonese fine dining experience, Flower Drum remains an enduring icon. Located in Market Lane, this institution has consistently delivered exquisite food and impeccable service for decades. From its famous Peking Duck to delicate dim sum and masterfully prepared seafood, every dish is a testament to culinary artistry. Arriving at Flower Drum with AR Tours elevates the occasion, ensuring you are greeted with the respect and attention that befits such a distinguished establishment.
