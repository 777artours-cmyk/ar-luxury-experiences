A Chauffeur's Guide to Melbourne's Best Restaurants

By AR Tours·June 29, 2026·10 minutes
Back to Blog

Introduction to Melbourne's Food Scene

Melbourne, the cultural capital of Australia, is renowned for its vibrant food scene. As a premier private chauffeur and tour company, AR Tours is well-versed in the city's best restaurants, cafes, and bars. Our expert chauffeurs have compiled a guide to Melbourne's top dining spots, ensuring that you experience the best of the city's culinary delights.

Fine Dining in Melbourne

Melbourne is home to a plethora of fine dining restaurants, each offering a unique culinary experience. Some of the top fine dining restaurants in Melbourne include:

  • Attica: A three-hat restaurant known for its innovative and seasonal menu
  • Brae: A two-hat restaurant located in the countryside, offering a focus on local and organic produce
  • Minamishima: A three-hat Japanese restaurant, renowned for its sushi and seafood

These restaurants offer an exceptional dining experience, with impeccable service and exquisite cuisine. Our chauffeurs can transport you to these restaurants in style and comfort, ensuring that you arrive feeling relaxed and ready to enjoy your meal.

Casual Eats in Melbourne

Melbourne is also home to a thriving casual dining scene, with a wide range of restaurants, cafes, and bars to choose from. Some popular casual eats in Melbourne include:

  • Lune Croissanterie: A renowned bakery and cafe, famous for its flaky and delicious croissants
  • Pellegrini's Espresso Bar: A historic cafe, serving up traditional Italian coffee and cuisine
  • Supernormal: A modern Asian restaurant, offering a range of small plates and share dishes

These casual eats offer a more relaxed dining experience, with a focus on fresh, local produce and creative cuisine. Our chauffeurs can recommend the best casual dining spots in Melbourne, tailored to your tastes and preferences.

Exploring Regional Victoria

Melbourne is the perfect base for exploring regional Victoria, with its stunning coastline, picturesque countryside, and world-class wineries. Our Great Ocean Road tour is a must-do for any visitor to Melbourne, offering breathtaking views of the Twelve Apostles and other iconic landmarks. Along the way, our chauffeurs can stop at some of the region's best restaurants and wineries, providing a truly immersive culinary experience.

Conclusion

In conclusion, Melbourne's food scene is a culinary paradise, with a wide range of dining options to suit every taste and budget.
