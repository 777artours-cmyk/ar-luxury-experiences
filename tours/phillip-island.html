Tour Overview

Watch one of the world's most unique natural wildlife migrations in absolute style. Every evening at sunset, hundreds of wild Little Penguins arrive from the ocean, shaking off the surf and waddling across Summerland Beach to their dune burrows. Our private Phillip Island Penguin Tour provides standard-setting hospitality and premium access to this magical event.

Your private tour begins with a relaxing afternoon drive out of Melbourne, stopping at historic Churchill Island Heritage Farm and the sweeping panoramic clifftops of the Nobbies. Walk the wooden boardwalks to view Seal Rocks and dramatic coastal blowholes before settling in at the premium Penguin Parade viewing platforms.

Enjoy door-to-door transit, curated dinner plans at Cowes coastal village, and expert guidance on the absolute best photography spots. Because we operate purely direct, bespoke custom adjustments are handled instantly by your professional private chauffeur.

Bespoke Itinerary Timeline

12:00 AEST
Bespoke Hotel Pickup
Your private chauffeur arrives in a premium luxury van or sedan. Enjoy a smooth 90-minute drive out of Melbourne's bustle towards Western Port Bay.
13:30 AEST
Churchill Island Heritage Farm
Step back in time at Victoria's first agricultural settlement. Walk through historic gardens, watch sheep-shearing demonstrations, and view magnificent Highland cattle.
15:00 AEST
Koala Conservation Centre
Walk along elevated boardwalks through native eucalyptus forests to view koalas in their natural habitat. Learn about conservation efforts protecting Phillip Island's unique ecosystems.
16:30 AEST
The Nobbies Clifftops & Seal Rocks
Walk the scenic boardwalks at Point Grant. View the dramatic rugged coastline, watch nesting seabirds, and look out over the ocean to spot Australia's largest fur seal colony at Seal Rocks.
18:00 AEST
Seaside Dinner at Cowes
Enjoy a wonderful dinner (own expense) at a premium boutique restaurant in the charming coastal village of Cowes, featuring direct views of the quiet northern bay.
19:30 AEST
The Sunset Penguin Parade
Take your premium reserved seats at Summerland Beach. As the sun sets, watch hundreds of little penguins emerge from the ocean and waddle directly past the boardwalks.
21:30 AEST
Comfortable Return to Melbourne
Relax inside the warm luxury cabin as your chauffeur navigates back to Melbourne. Quiet refreshments are available onboard during the peaceful drive.
