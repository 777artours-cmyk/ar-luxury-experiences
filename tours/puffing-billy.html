Tour Overview

Step back in time to an era of historic engineering and untouched natural beauty. Tucked just east of Melbourne CBD, the Dandenong Ranges are a sanctuary of massive, ancient Mountain Ash forests, cool lush fern gullies, and charming Tudor-style villages. Our private Dandenong Ranges experience centers around Australia's favorite operating steam train, Puffing Billy.

Enjoy a first-class private cabin ride on Puffing Billy as it chugs along its historic narrow-gauge track from Belgrave to Menzies Creek, crossing landmark timber trestle bridges. Afterwards, your private chauffeur takes you deep into the temperate rainforest of Sherbrooke Forest, the high-altitude lookouts of Silvan Reservoir, and boutique cafes in the mountain villages.

Travel in style and premium luxury in our hand-selected fleet. Complete with an expert local professional chauffeur, refreshing cold beverages, and tailored stops, this is Victoria seen the right way—your way.

Bespoke Itinerary Timeline

09:00 AEST
Bespoke Hotel Pickup
Your private chauffeur arrives in a sparkling clean Luxury Vehicle, welcoming you onboard with chilled spring water. Settle in for a smooth drive towards the blue-hued Dandenong Ranges.
10:00 AEST
Puffing Billy Steam Train Ride
Board the historic narrow-gauge steam train. Sit back and relax in first-class passenger comfort as the steam engine chugs through towering trees and over the landmark timber trestle bridge.
11:30 AEST
Dandenong Ranges Botanic Garden
Stroll along manicured gravel paths showcasing thousands of colorful rhododendrons, azaleas, and majestic proteas set against giant, towering mountain ash.
13:00 AEST
Traditional Devonshire Lunch
Stop at a charming cafe in a cozy Tudor-style mountain village. Enjoy a wonderful gourmet lunch and traditional warm scones with fresh cream and local berry jam.
14:30 AEST
Sherbrooke Forest Canopy Walk
Take a guided, low-impact walk through the ancient temperate rainforest of Sherbrooke Forest. Listen out for the mimicry calls of local wild lyrebirds in the ferns.
16:00 AEST
Silvan Reservoir Scenic Lookout
Visit the panoramic lookout overlooking the pristine waters of Silvan Reservoir, learning the history of Melbourne's high-altitude water catchment network.
17:00 AEST
Comfortable Return to Melbourne
Relax inside the warm luxury cabin as your chauffeur navigates back to Melbourne CBD, dropping you directly at your hotel or residence by 18:30.
