Tour Overview

Unlock the true culture of Australia's cultural capital. Famous for its secret laneways, heritage Victorian architecture, world-leading coffee, and electric street art scene, Melbourne is best explored with a local expert. Our private Melbourne City Discovery Tour is fully flexible and tailors specifically to your exact interests.

Forget crowded tour buses that only stick to major roads. Your private professional chauffeur guides you through narrow laneways, historic quarters, and trendy bayside beaches. Bespoke Itinerary Timeline

Hour 1
Bespoke Hotel Pickup & Laneways
Your private chauffeur arrives at your CBD hotel or residence. Start by exploring the famous city laneways, Flinders Street Station, and viewing the electric graffiti art at Hosier Lane.
Hour 2
Specialty Coffee Flight
Stop at a world-class hidden roastery. Enjoy a curated specialty coffee tasting flight (included), learning what makes Melbourne's coffee culture globally legendary.
Hour 3
Fitzroy & Carlton Quarters
Drive through the historic Victorian terrace streets of Carlton and Fitzroy. Explore boutique heritage quarters, local design shops, and the beautiful Royal Exhibition Building.
Hour 4
Bayside Beaches & Brighton Boxes
Head south along the bay esplanade. Stop at St Kilda beach and visit the iconic Brighton Beach to view and photograph the world-famous colored wooden bathing boxes.
Hour 5
Shrine of Remembrance & Southbank
Enjoy high-altitude panoramic views of the city skyline from the balcony of the historic Shrine of Remembrance, before cruising through Southbank arts precinct.
Hour 6
Bespoke Hotel Drop-off
Your private chauffeur returns you safely back to your hotel or residence, providing excellent recommendations for Melbourne's best hidden fine dining spots for the evening.
