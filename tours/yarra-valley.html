Tour Overview

Indulge in a sensory journey through the rolling, vineyard-draped hills of the Yarra Valley, just an hour's comfortable drive from Melbourne CBD. Known globally for its award-winning Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and revolutionary gin distilleries, this private luxury experience offers tailored entry into the valley's most exclusive boutique estates.

Forget large bus schedules and rigid timelines. With your private designated chauffeur at the wheel, you dictate the pace. Visit historic cellars like Yering Station, enjoy private tutored tastings at Dominique Portet, and indulge in a long, scenic gourmet winery lunch overlooking the vines.

Whether you are a certified wine connoisseur or looking for a relaxing escape with friends, we curate the estates, book the tasting slots, and manage every logistics detail directly. All booking inquiries link to our direct engine, ensuring premium, secure hospitality with no middleman platforms.
