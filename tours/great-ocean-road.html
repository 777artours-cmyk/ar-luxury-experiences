Tour Overview

The iconic Great Ocean Road is widely celebrated as one of the world's most spectacular coastal drives. However, traveling the standard route alongside dozens of tourist coaches can detract from the majestic serenity of the landscape. Our signature reverse-direction private tour turns this journey on its head.

By departing early and traveling directly to the Twelve Apostles in the morning, you experience Australia's most famous limestone sea stacks in peaceful tranquility. Witness the dramatic cliffs and ocean spires illuminated by the soft morning light before the crowds arrive, then leisurely trace the coastline back to Melbourne.

Travel in style and premium luxury in our hand-selected fleet. Complete with an expert local professional chauffeur, refreshing cold beverages, and tailored stops, this is Victoria seen the right way—your way.

Bespoke Itinerary Timeline

07:00 AEST
Bespoke Hotel Pickup
Your dedicated chauffeur arrives at your Melbourne CBD hotel or residence in a sparkling clean, air-conditioned luxury vehicle. Settle in for a smooth journey inland directly towards the coast.
10:00 AEST
Twelve Apostles (Crowd-Free)
Arrive at the famous Port Campbell National Park. Witness the majestic limestone sea stacks rising from the Southern Ocean in the serene morning air, entirely free from tourist coach crowds.
11:30 AEST
Loch Ard Gorge & Shipwreck Coast
Walk down into the spectacular beach enclave of Loch Ard Gorge. Learn the historic tales of the clipper ship Loch Ard and explore the neighboring razorback formations.
13:00 AEST
Apollo Bay Gourmet Lunch
Stop at the beautiful seaside town of Apollo Bay. Enjoy a leisurely gourmet lunch (own expense) overlooking the ocean, with fresh local seafood highly recommended.
15:00 AEST
Otway Rainforest & Wild Koalas
Take a guided walk among towering ancient mountain ash and giant tree ferns. Afterwards, stop at Kennett River to spot wild koalas sleeping high in the eucalyptus branches.
18:00 AEST
Lorne Coastal Drive
Wind along the true coastal sections of the road. Stop at the picturesque resort town of Lorne and grab photos beneath the Memorial Arch entry gateway.
21:00 AEST
Return Transfer to Melbourne
Relax in the premium leather seats as your chauffeur guides the vehicle back into Melbourne, dropping you safely back to your hotel or CBD apartment.
